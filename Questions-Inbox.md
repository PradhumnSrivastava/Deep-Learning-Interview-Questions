
39. What is cohort analysis in Data Science? Ans- Cohort analysis groups individuals who share a characteristic or experience within a defined period and tracks their behavior over time, such as customer retention after signup.

40. What is time series data, and how is it different from cross-sectional data? Ans- Time series data consists of observations recorded over time, whereas cross-sectional data captures observations across different individuals or entities at a particular point in time.

41. What is seasonality in time series analysis? Ans- Seasonality refers to recurring patterns that occur at regular intervals, such as increased retail sales during festivals or higher electricity demand during particular seasons.

42. What is concept drift in a Data Science system? Ans- Concept drift occurs when the relationship between input features and the target variable changes over time, potentially reducing the predictive performance of a deployed model.

43. What is a data pipeline, and why is it important? Ans- A data pipeline is a sequence of automated steps that moves and transforms data from source systems to destinations for storage, analysis, reporting, or machine learning.

44. What is the difference between ETL and ELT? Ans- ETL extracts data, transforms it, and then loads it into the destination, while ELT extracts and loads data first and performs transformations within the destination system.

45. What is data governance in Data Science? Ans- Data governance is the framework of policies, roles, standards, and procedures used to manage data quality, ownership, security, privacy, and appropriate usage.

46. What is data anonymization, and why is it important? Ans- Data anonymization transforms data to reduce the possibility of identifying individuals, helping protect privacy when datasets are used for analysis or research.

47. What is the difference between a data lake and a data warehouse? Ans- A data lake stores large volumes of raw data in various formats, while a data warehouse stores structured and processed data optimized for reporting and analytical queries.

48. What is the role of a Data Scientist in an organization? Ans- A Data Scientist defines analytical problems, explores data, develops statistical or machine learning models, evaluates results, and communicates actionable insights to stakeholders.

49. What is an A/B test, and how is it used in Data Science? Ans- An A/B test compares two versions of a product, feature, or experience by assigning users to different groups and measuring outcomes to estimate the effect of a change.

50. What is responsible Data Science? Ans- Responsible Data Science involves developing and using data-driven systems with attention to fairness, privacy, transparency, accountability, security, and potential societal impacts.
